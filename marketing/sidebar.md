GridPop is a daily number puzzle played right here in the feed. Drop pieces on a 4x4 board: every tile under a piece goes up by one. Fill a row or column with the same number and it pops. Build the target board in as few pieces as you can, but don't let a tile reach 7. A new puzzle is posted every day at midnight Eastern in Easy, Normal and Hard. ## Widget: How to play

- Match the target board in as few pieces as you can.
- Every tile under a piece goes up by one.
- Fill a row or column with the same number to pop it.
- A tile that reaches 7 ends the run.
- Tap to place, double-tap to rotate, drag to move.
- Keyboard: Space to place, A or D to rotate, arrow keys to move.

## Widget: Daily puzzle

- New puzzle: every day, midnight Eastern
- Levels: Easy (3 pieces), Normal (4–5), Hard (6–7)
- Scored run: your first solve each day; replays are practice
- Score: pieces placed (fewer is better)
- Ties: fewer hints, then fewer restarts, then faster time
- Streak: one per level
